    Case definitions, coverage, and sources

    A mapped coastal storm has an identified extratropical coastal phase and independently documented U.S. effects. Strict nor’easters also meet the research criteria for coastal track, northeasterly flow or authoritative storm identification, and impacts attributed to that coastal phase. The broader class includes cases with insufficient evidence to establish a strict classification.

    Each point represents a supported pressure-center location at a specific time. Lines connect adjacent fixes with comparable timing, and pressure summaries use the fixes shown. The case detail identifies the track method and links to its weather and impact evidence. The storm date range describes the case, which can extend beyond the plotted fixes.

    Coverage varies by winter, and unresolved cases and cases without a supported track are outside this map sample. The filters describe the mapped cases. Calculating a historical occurrence rate requires a separate, consistently reviewed set of storm and quiet periods.
